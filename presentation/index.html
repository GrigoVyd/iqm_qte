① Part 1 · W states · routing 04 / 10

Same circuit. Same hardware. Where on the chip you put it matters.

The story. A W-state circuit is a 1-D chain — qubits talk only to their neighbours. The IQM Selector is brilliant for arbitrary circuits but doesn't exploit that, so its layout can zig-zag and the transpiler inserts SWAPs.

Our beam-search Hamiltonian-path router scores chains by multiplicative log-fidelity:

𝓛 = Σ log FRO + Σ log FCZ + Σ log PT₁(Δt) + Σ log PT₂(Δt)
n=15 on GarnetFz⟨Wx⟩σ above 0
IQM Qubit Selector0.31+0.085+14σ
Beam-search chain0.68+0.114+31σ

Free 2× fidelity — same chip, just a router that knows the circuit's shape.

Both layouts on the actual Garnet chip — the right one (beam-search) follows a clean Hamiltonian path; the left one's chain has to jump diagonally.

① Part 1 · W states · witness 05 / 10

With the right routing — our first witness, on both devices.

What's a W state? One excitation, distributed coherently across n qubits:

|10⋯00⟩ + |01⋯00⟩ + ⋯ + |00⋯01⟩ ⁄ √n

The trap. If you only measure in the Z basis, this looks identical to a *classical* random mixture: one qubit hot, the rest cold. To prove the state is actually quantum, we need a witness that sees coherence — the pairwise X-correlator:

Quantum ⟨Xi Xj⟩ = 2/n on |Wn⟩ — coherence shows up
Classical ⟨Xi Xj⟩ = 0 on a single-excitation mixture

Hardware: every (device, n) gives the witness +25σ to +60σ above the classical 0.

Fz and ⟨Wx⟩ vs n · dashed black: ideal 2/n · red dotted: classical bound 0

③ Part 3 · Routed Bell pair · fourth witness 09 / 10

Cluster states are also a resource — route a Bell pair across the chip.

The protocol. Pick a path A — q₁ — … — B through the cluster. Measure all internals in X. What remains on (A, B) is locally equivalent to |Φ⁺⟩, modulo a Pauli byproduct that depends on the internal outcomes.

F = (1 + ⟨XX⟩c + ⟨YY⟩c + ⟨ZZ⟩c) / 4

Outlook · early result 10 / 11

Let the hardware tune the router itself.

The routing loss has four free weights:

𝓛 = wCZ·Σlog FCZ + wRO·Σlog FRO + wT₁·Σlog PT₁ + wT₂·Σlog PT₂

Equal weights is just a guess. So we ran a Nelder–Mead search — each sample's chosen chain executed on Emerald, Fz to |W30⟩ as the objective.
